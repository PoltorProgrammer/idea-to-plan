# Question bank by stage

Ideas for building rounds. **Do not paste verbatim**: pick questions that truly change a decision, rephrase them with the user's context, and turn closed ones into A/B/C options with pros and cons.

## Stage 2 · Purpose and success
- What concrete problem does this solve, and who suffers it today?
- What do people do now to solve it? What fails about that?
- What happens if nothing is done?
- Is it for personal use, a team, clients, the public?
- What drives it: saving time, earning money, learning, a personal need, validating an idea?
- In 3 months, what must be true to say "it was worth it"?
- How much weekly effort can you realistically give it?

## Stage 3 · Scope and limits
- If you could only deliver 3 things, which?
- What stays out even if it is a good idea? (non-goals)
- Is there a deadline, and is it real or self-imposed?
- Maximum budget and for what?
- Who works on this and with what skills? What do you want to learn and what to delegate?
- Any personal data, sensitive information or legal requirements (privacy, contracts, licenses)?
- What do you depend on that you do not control (third parties, approvals, platforms)?

## Stage 4 · Phases and goals
- What is the smallest version that would already be useful or validate the idea?
- How would you verify a phase is done without debate? (acceptance criterion)
- Where would you like to stop and decide whether to continue, change or abandon?
- Few long phases or many short ones?
- Any external milestones (launch, demo, meeting) fixing dates?
- What could wait for a second version?

## Stage 5 · Approach and tools
General:
- Any tools or technologies you already master or your environment already uses?
- Simplest to maintain, or most powerful and scalable?
- Acceptable recurring cost (subscriptions, hosting, APIs)?
- Does being able to switch provider later matter?

If software (adapt layers to what applies):
- What form: web, mobile, desktop, CLI, bot, API, automation?
- Language and framework: any already known? Which ecosystem has the best documentation and support?
- Data: what is stored, how much, with what relations? Relational, document, files?
- Users and auth: one person or many? Accounts, roles?
- AI or external services: which part needs a model or third-party API? Acceptable latency and cost?
- Deployment: local, own server, PaaS, cloud? Where must data live?
- Testing and quality: what level of automated testing is reasonable?
- Maintenance: who maintains it once built?

If not software:
- What resources, tools or people do you need? Which do you already have?
- What method (research, prototype, pilot, batch production)?
- What providers or alternatives exist and what do they cost?

## Stage 6 · Human / agent split
- Which accounts, accesses or payments must you handle personally?
- Which tests need a real person or physical device?
- How often do you want to review the agent's work: each step, each phase, only at the end?
- Which tasks do you prefer doing yourself even if the agent could?
- What must the agent never do without your permission (delete, publish, spend, send)?

## Stage 7 · Risks and assumptions
- If it failed, what would be the most likely cause?
- Which part scares you or feels most uncertain?
- Which assumption, if false, would sink the project?
- What depends on a single person, tool or provider?
- What if your available time halves?

## Calibration questions (any stage)
- Is this a real requirement or a preference?
- Which decision in this round would be most expensive to change later?
- Does anything you said earlier clash with this answer?
